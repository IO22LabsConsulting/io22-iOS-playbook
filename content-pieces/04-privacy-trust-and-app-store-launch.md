# Privacy, Trust, and App Store Launch Readiness

Most launch delays are not technical. They are trust and compliance gaps discovered too late.

## Build Trust Before You Ask for Scale

Users need to understand three things quickly:

1. What data you collect
2. Why you collect it
3. How they can control or delete it

This should be visible in-app, not hidden only in legal pages.

## Practical Privacy Controls

Include these by default:

- Permissions requested in context, not all at once
- Consent-gated diagnostics and telemetry
- Simple export and delete actions in settings
- Clear retention explanations

If your app uses ML outputs, include plain-language model limitations and fallback behavior.

## Launch Readiness Signals

Before App Store submission, verify:

- Crash-free sessions are at target
- Core flows tested on oldest supported device
- Policy and support links are live
- App Store privacy questionnaire matches reality
- Incident response ownership is defined

## The Strategic Advantage

Trust-forward products convert better and retain longer.
When users feel in control of their data and understand app behavior, they are more willing to build habits with your product.

Launch quality is not only code quality. It is expectation quality.
